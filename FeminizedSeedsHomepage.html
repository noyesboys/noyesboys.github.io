The Noyes Boys story

Great cannabis starts with great genetics.

Founded with a passion for cannabis genetics and a commitment to quality, The Noyes Boys Seed Bank has been serving Canadian growers since 2015.

Our breeding program focuses on plant structure and resilience first, knowing that potency and flavor naturally follow. We breed for Canadian Purples, dense but well-spaced internodes, mildew resistance and ease of growth, for first-time growers and seasoned cultivators alike.
